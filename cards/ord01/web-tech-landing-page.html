📦 SCOPE: LANDING PAGE
One page built to get calls and form fills for one main offer. No extra pages, no blog.

1. 📋 ONBOARDING FORM REVIEW

  • Onboarding form reviewed: business details, the main service or offer, service area

  • Logo received in a usable format

  • Photos received, or stock photos agreed with the client

  • Anything missing commented and sent to Luis to collect (@mention him in a contact note)

2. 🔐 DOMAIN & ACCESS

  • Confirmed who owns the domain and where it's registered

  • Registrar or DNS access received

  • Checked whether the client uses email on this domain, and recorded the current MX records in a comment before changing anything

⚠️ Changing DNS can break the client's email. Record the MX records before you touch anything, and confirm email still works after launch.

3. 🛠️ BUILD

  • Headline and offer above the fold

  • Services, trust section (reviews, years in business, licensing), and service area

  • Click-to-call phone number at the top and bottom of the page

  • Contact form built, and submissions reach the client and the CRM

  • Business name, address, and phone match the onboarding form and the Google Business Profile

  • Page title and meta description written

  • Favicon and privacy policy in place

  • Checked on mobile, tablet, and desktop

4. 👀 CLIENT REVIEW

  • Preview link sent to the client

  • Revisions completed (comment each round with the date)

  • Client approval received in writing, with the date commented here

5. 🌐 LAUNCH

  • Domain connected and the page loads on it

  • SSL active (https with no warnings)

  • www and non-www both point to the same page

  • Client email still sending and receiving after the DNS change

  • Contact form tested live, and the test submission arrived

6. 📈 AFTER LAUNCH

  • Analytics installed and recording visits

  • Google Search Console set up

  • Website link on the Google Business Profile updated

  • Live URL commented here, and Luis @mentioned in a contact note so he can tell the client

✅ WHEN DONE
Every line crossed out? Set this card to Completed. Website is setup only, so no review card is created.
