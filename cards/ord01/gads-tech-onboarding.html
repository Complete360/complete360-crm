🚀 BUILD ONLY
This card covers getting the campaign live. Completing it creates the account review cards for you and Luis.

📌 STATUS

  • Scheduled: waiting on Luis's handoff. He'll @mention you in a contact note when access and billing are in.

  • In Progress: working the checklist. When you start, set the due date to your target launch date.

  • Blocked/Delayed: waiting on the client, verification, or ad approval (set Exception)

  • Completed: every line below is crossed out

✏️ HOW TO USE THIS CHECKLIST
When you finish a line, highlight it and press the S (strikethrough) button so it looks like this. Don't delete lines. Crossed-out lines are the record of what's done.

1. 🏗️ ACCOUNT SETUP

  • Google Ads account created or linked to our manager account

  • Client's payment method active on the account

  • Advertiser verification completed, if Google requested it

  • Account time zone and currency correct

2. 📈 CONVERSION TRACKING

  • Call tracking set up (calls from ads and calls from the website)

  • Form submissions tracked as conversions

  • Google tag or GA4 installed on the landing page and verified

  • Test conversion recorded for each conversion action

3. 🎯 CAMPAIGN BUILD

  • Campaign structure set up by service (one ad group per main service)

  • Keywords added for each service, with match types set

  • Negative keyword list added (jobs, DIY, free, and services they don't offer)

  • Locations set to the service area, targeting people in the area

  • Ad schedule matches the hours the client answers the phone

4. ✍️ ADS & ASSETS

  • Responsive search ads written for each ad group

  • Call asset with the right phone number

  • Location asset linked to the Google Business Profile, if they have one

  • Sitelinks and callouts added

5. 💰 BUDGET & BIDDING

  • Daily budget set to match the monthly budget that was sold

  • Bidding strategy set

6. 🚀 LAUNCH

  • Ads approved and serving (no disapprovals)

  • Landing page loads, form works, phone number correct

  • Comment the launch date and the daily budget on this card

⚠️ Billing and advertiser verification are the long poles. If either stalls, set Blocked/Delayed, comment the status, and @mention Luis in a contact note so he can chase the client.

✅ WHEN DONE
Every line crossed out? Set this card to Completed. That creates the account review cards for you and Luis, and Luis is notified automatically.
