📦 SCOPE: PREMIUM
Everything in Pro, plus a custom design, written copy, extra features the client asked for, and speed tuning.

1. 📋 ONBOARDING FORM REVIEW & KICKOFF

  • Onboarding form reviewed: business details, full service list, every service area, brand colors, and sites the client likes

  • Kickoff call held with the client to agree on look, pages, and features (comment the notes here)

  • Page list and feature list confirmed in writing before design starts

  • Logo and brand files received

  • Photos received, or a photo plan agreed (stock, client shoot, or both)

  • Anything missing commented and sent to Luis to collect (@mention him in a contact note)

2. 🔐 DOMAIN & ACCESS

  • Confirmed who owns the domain and where it's registered

  • Registrar or DNS access received

  • Existing website noted, if there is one, with a full list of pages that need redirects

  • Checked whether the client uses email on this domain, and recorded the current MX records in a comment before changing anything

  • Access received for any outside tools the site connects to (booking, payments, chat)

⚠️ Changing DNS can break the client's email. Record the MX records before you touch anything, and confirm email still works after launch.

3. 🎨 DESIGN & COPY

  • Homepage design mockup sent to the client and approved before building the rest

  • Copy written for every page in the client's tone

  • Copy approved by the client

4. 🛠️ BUILD

  • Core pages built: Home, About, Services overview, Contact

  • One page for each service

  • One page for each service area

  • Reviews or testimonials section, and a gallery of work

  • Extra features built and tested (booking, chat, payments, or whatever was agreed at kickoff)

  • Business name, address, and phone match the onboarding form and the Google Business Profile

  • Click-to-call phone number on every page

  • Contact form built, and submissions reach the client and the CRM

  • Page titles, meta descriptions, and headings written for each page around the service and city

  • Pages linked to each other (services to areas, areas to contact)

  • Images compressed and page speed checked

  • Favicon, privacy policy, and footer details in place

  • Checked on mobile, tablet, and desktop

5. 👀 CLIENT REVIEW

  • Preview link sent to the client

  • Revisions completed (comment each round with the date)

  • Client approval received in writing, with the date commented here

6. 🌐 LAUNCH

  • Domain connected and the site loads on it

  • SSL active (https with no warnings)

  • www and non-www both point to the same site

  • Old site pages redirected, if there was an old site

  • Client email still sending and receiving after the DNS change

  • Contact form and every extra feature tested live

7. 📈 AFTER LAUNCH

  • Google Search Console set up and the sitemap submitted

  • Analytics installed, with form submissions, phone clicks, and bookings tracked

  • Website link on the Google Business Profile updated

  • Short walkthrough given to the client on how to request changes

  • Live URL commented here, and Luis @mentioned in a contact note so he can tell the client

✅ WHEN DONE
Every line crossed out? Set this card to Completed. Website is setup only, so no review card is created.
